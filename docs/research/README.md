# Research

This directory contains the research foundation for the Multimodal Deepfake Detection System.

## Research areas

1. [Literature Review](literature-review.md) — papers, datasets, methods, and findings that establish the state of the art.
2. [Research Gap](research-gap.md) — limitations in current work that our project will investigate.
3. [Proposed Novelty](proposed-novelty.md) — what we intend to add, without claiming novelty until experiments support it.
4. [Research Questions](research-questions.md) — questions and hypotheses that drive the experiments.
5. [Dataset Strategy](dataset-strategy.md) — dataset selection, leakage control, splits, and cross-dataset evaluation.
6. [Experimental Protocol](experimental-protocol.md) — baselines, fusion experiments, robustness tests, ablations, and metrics.
7. [Research Roadmap](research-roadmap.md) — research milestones from literature review through the paper.

## Research principle

The project will distinguish clearly between established methods, engineering choices, proposed contributions, and experimentally demonstrated findings. No benchmark result or novelty claim will be made before the corresponding experiment is completed and documented.
